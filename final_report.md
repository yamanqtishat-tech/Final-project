# Online Education Platform

## Final Executive Summary

### 1. Business Question

The main business question of this project is:

**How can the online education platform use student purchasing data to understand student behavior and improve revenue?**

The analysis focuses on course performance, student purchasing activity, spending behavior, and student segments in order to identify opportunities for improving revenue and student engagement.

---

### 2. Key Business Insights

**Insight 1 — Data Analysis generated the highest course revenue**

Data Analysis was the strongest revenue-generating course, with total revenue of **5,040** from **84 purchases**. This indicates strong demand for the course and makes it an important product for the platform to monitor and develop.

**Insight 2 — More frequent purchases are strongly associated with higher student spending**

The analysis found a very strong positive relationship between purchase frequency and total student spending. Students who make more purchases generally generate higher total revenue for the platform.

**Insight 3 — Student behavior differs across customer segments**

The RFM and K-Means analysis identified **four different student segments** with different purchasing behaviors. Segment 0 was the largest revenue contributor, generating **9,600**, representing **44.07%** of total segment revenue.

**Insight 4 — Data Analysis purchasers showed higher overall spending**

Students who purchased the Data Analysis course had an average total spending of **190.17**, compared with **113.45** among students who did not purchase the course.

---

### 3. Hypothesis Test Result

The analysis examined whether students who purchased the Data Analysis course had different average total spending from students who did not purchase it.

The results showed that **Data Analysis purchasers spent significantly more on average than students who did not purchase the course**.

This suggests that students interested in Data Analysis may represent a valuable customer group for additional courses, cross-selling, and targeted offers.

---

### 4. Additional Analysis — RFM and K-Means

RFM analysis was used to understand students based on:

* How recently they purchased.
* How frequently they purchased.
* How much they spent.

K-Means then grouped students into four behavioral segments.

The largest revenue contribution came from **Segment 0**, which generated **9,600 (44.07%)** of segment revenue. Segment 3 had the highest average purchase frequency and the highest average monetary value.

This segmentation provides a basis for creating different engagement and retention strategies for different student groups rather than treating all students in the same way.

---

### 5. Recommendations

**Recommendation 1 — Develop and promote the Data Analysis course**

Because Data Analysis generated the highest course revenue and its purchasers showed higher average spending, the platform should continue promoting this course and consider related course bundles or cross-selling opportunities.

**Recommendation 2 — Use student segments for targeted campaigns**

Different student segments show different purchasing behaviors. The platform should create targeted campaigns based on student activity, such as engagement offers for less-active students and additional-course recommendations for frequent and high-value students.

**Recommendation 3 — Focus on increasing purchase frequency**

Because purchase frequency is strongly associated with total student spending, the platform should encourage existing students to purchase additional courses through course bundles, personalized recommendations, and follow-up promotions.

---

### 6. Conclusion

The analysis shows that student purchasing behavior provides useful information for improving the business performance of an online education platform.

Course performance, student spending, purchase frequency, and behavioral segmentation can be combined to identify valuable customer groups and opportunities for revenue growth.

The interactive Streamlit dashboard developed in Week 9 allows decision-makers to explore these results through date and course filters and provides a practical way to monitor the platform's performance.

**Note:** The dataset used in this project is a simulated dataset created for educational and portfolio purposes. The findings demonstrate the analytical approach and should not be interpreted as results from actual company transactions.
